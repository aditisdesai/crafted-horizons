Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how your information is used and protected.

Who we are

Crafted Horizons is operated by Aditi as an independent travel advisor based in the UK. We provide travel planning and booking support using trusted travel partners.

What information we collect

We only collect information that is necessary to respond to enquiries and arrange travel. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Travel preferences and enquiry details
  • Any information you choose to share when contacting us

How your information is used

Your information is used solely to:

  • Respond to travel enquiries
  • Provide travel planning and booking support
  • Communicate with you about your request

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Sharing your information

Where necessary to arrange travel, your information may be shared with trusted third-party travel suppliers (such as airlines, hotels, tour operators, or booking platforms).

These suppliers are responsible for handling your data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

How your data is protected

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information and only use reputable platforms and partners for communication and bookings.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Basic analytics may be used to understand site usage, but no personal information is collected for marketing purposes.

Contact
